# Sexual Harassment Prevention Policy

## Policy Statement

EKAP is committed to providing a work environment free from all forms of harassment, discrimination, and retaliation. Sexual harassment in the workplace is illegal, violates company policy, and will not be tolerated under any circumstances. This policy applies to all employees, contractors, vendors, and visitors at all EKAP locations and company-sponsored events.

## Definitions

### Quid Pro Quo Harassment
Quid pro quo harassment occurs when submission to or rejection of unwelcome sexual conduct is used as the basis for employment decisions affecting an individual. Examples include: a supervisor suggesting that an employee's promotion depends on accepting a date, or threatening negative performance reviews if sexual advances are refused.

### Hostile Work Environment Harassment
A hostile work environment exists when unwelcome conduct of a sexual nature is sufficiently severe or pervasive to unreasonably interfere with an individual's work performance or create an intimidating, hostile, or offensive working environment. This conduct can be physical, verbal, or visual.

## Prohibited Conduct

The following behaviors are strictly prohibited:

- Unwanted physical contact, touching, or invasion of personal space of a sexual nature
- Sexually explicit or suggestive comments, jokes, or conversations
- Displaying, sharing, or distributing sexually explicit images, videos, or materials
- Sending unwanted sexually suggestive messages, emails, or social media communications
- Making sexual propositions or repeated unwanted requests for dates
- Commenting on a person's body, appearance, or clothing in a sexual manner
- Creating or sharing content that demeans individuals based on gender or sexuality
- Stalking, following, or persistently pursuing a coworker despite clear disinterest

## Reporting Procedures

Employees who experience or witness sexual harassment are strongly encouraged to report it promptly. EKAP offers multiple reporting channels to ensure accessibility and comfort:

**HR Hotline:** Call 1-800-HR-EKAP (1-800-47-3527) — available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Reports may be made anonymously.

**Anonymous Online Portal:** Submit a confidential report at hr.ekap.internal/report — no login required for anonymous submissions.

**Direct HR Contact:** Contact any member of the HR department directly by email at hrteam@ekap.com or in person at any HR office location.

**Manager Escalation:** If comfortable doing so, employees may report to their direct manager or any other manager. However, managers who receive reports are required by policy to escalate to HR within 24 hours.

Employees are not required to confront the harasser before making a report.

## Investigation Process

Upon receipt of a complaint, HR will:

1. Acknowledge the report within one business day
2. Assign a trained investigator (internal or external) within three business days
3. Conduct a thorough, impartial investigation including interviews with all relevant parties
4. Maintain documentation of all investigative steps
5. Complete the investigation within 30 calendar days whenever possible
6. Communicate the outcome to the reporting party and the subject of the complaint

## Confidentiality

EKAP will maintain the confidentiality of harassment complaints to the greatest extent possible consistent with conducting a thorough investigation and taking appropriate corrective action. Information will be shared only on a need-to-know basis.

## Anti-Retaliation

Retaliation against any employee who reports sexual harassment, participates in an investigation, or opposes harassment in good faith is strictly prohibited and constitutes a separate policy violation subject to disciplinary action, up to and including termination. Employees who believe they have experienced retaliation should report it immediately using any of the channels listed above.

## Consequences

Employees found to have engaged in sexual harassment will be subject to disciplinary action commensurate with the severity of the conduct, up to and including immediate termination. EKAP will also take appropriate action against any manager who fails to act on a report of harassment brought to their attention.

## Support Resources

EKAP recognizes that experiencing harassment can be traumatic. The following resources are available to all employees at no cost:

**Employee Assistance Program (EAP):** Call 1-800-EAP-HELP (1-800-327-4357) for confidential counseling, legal consultation, and crisis support — available 24/7.

**EEOC Information:** The U.S. Equal Employment Opportunity Commission can be reached at 1-800-669-4000 for information about your legal rights.

All employees will receive mandatory sexual harassment prevention training annually.
