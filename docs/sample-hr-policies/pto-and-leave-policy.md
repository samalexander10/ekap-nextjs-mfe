# PTO and Leave Policy

## Overview

EKAP values work-life balance and offers a comprehensive suite of paid and unpaid leave programs. This policy applies to all full-time employees. Part-time employees are eligible for prorated benefits where noted. Leave requests should be submitted through the HR Self-Service Portal or by contacting your HR Business Partner.

## Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual

PTO is earned based on length of continuous service and accrues each pay period:

| Years of Service | Annual PTO Days | Accrual Per Pay Period (Biweekly) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 – 2 years | 15 days | 0.577 days |
| 2 – 5 years | 18 days | 0.692 days |
| 5+ years | 22 days | 0.846 days |

PTO begins accruing on the first day of employment. Employees may carry over up to 10 days of unused PTO into the following calendar year. Accrued but unused PTO above the carryover cap is forfeited at year-end. Upon separation from the company, accrued and unused PTO is paid out in accordance with applicable state law.

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is provided separately from PTO. Employees receive 10 sick days per calendar year, available in full on January 1 (or upon hire for new employees on a prorated basis). Sick leave may be used for the employee's own illness or injury, medical appointments, or to care for an ill immediate family member. Sick leave does not carry over and has no cash-out value upon separation.

## Family and Medical Leave (FMLA)

EKAP complies fully with the federal Family and Medical Leave Act. Eligible employees may take up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave per year for qualifying reasons including:

- The birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child
- The serious health condition of the employee
- The serious health condition of an immediate family member (spouse, child, or parent)
- Qualifying exigencies related to a family member's military service

**Eligibility:** Employees must have worked for EKAP for at least 12 months and have logged at least 1,250 hours in the 12 months preceding the leave. FMLA leave runs concurrently with any applicable PTO or sick leave.

## Parental Leave

EKAP provides paid parental leave beyond FMLA for employees welcoming a new child:

- **Primary Caregiver:** 12 weeks of paid leave at 100% of base salary
- **Secondary Caregiver:** 4 weeks of paid leave at 100% of base salary

Parental leave must be taken within the first 12 months of the child's birth, adoption, or foster placement. Employees must have completed 6 months of continuous employment to be eligible for paid parental leave. Paid parental leave runs concurrently with FMLA leave where applicable.

## Bereavement Leave

Employees may take paid bereavement leave following the death of a family member:

- **Immediate family** (spouse, domestic partner, child, parent, sibling, grandparent, grandchild, or parent-in-law): 3 days paid leave
- **Extended family** (aunt, uncle, niece, nephew, cousin, or close friend): 1 day paid leave

Additional unpaid leave may be granted at the manager's discretion. Documentation may be requested.

## Jury Duty

EKAP supports employees fulfilling their civic duty. Employees summoned for jury duty will receive their full base salary for up to 2 weeks. For jury service extending beyond 2 weeks, employees may use PTO or take unpaid leave. Employees must provide documentation of jury service and are expected to return to work on any day they are released early.

## Company Holidays

EKAP observes 11 company holidays per year:

1. New Year's Day
2. Martin Luther King Jr. Day
3. Presidents' Day
4. Memorial Day
5. Juneteenth
6. Independence Day
7. Labor Day
8. Thanksgiving Day
9. Day After Thanksgiving
10. Christmas Eve
11. Christmas Day

When a holiday falls on a weekend, the observed day will be the preceding Friday or following Monday as announced by HR each year.

For questions about leave balances or to request leave, contact HR at hrteam@ekap.com or use the HR Self-Service Portal.
