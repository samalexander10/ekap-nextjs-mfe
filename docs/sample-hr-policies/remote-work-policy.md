# Remote Work Policy

## Overview

EKAP supports flexible work arrangements that enable employees to perform their best work while maintaining the collaboration and connection that drives our success. This policy establishes the guidelines and expectations for employees working remotely, whether full-time remote or in a hybrid capacity. Adherence to this policy is required for all employees who work outside an EKAP office.

## Eligibility

Not all roles are suitable for remote work. Eligibility is determined by two criteria:

1. **Role Approval:** The employee's position must be designated as remote-eligible by their department head and HR. Roles requiring physical presence, hands-on work, or access to on-site equipment are generally not eligible.

2. **Employment Tenure:** Employees must have completed a minimum of 6 months of continuous employment before being considered for a remote work arrangement. New hires typically work on-site during their initial onboarding period.

Employees wishing to transition to a remote or hybrid arrangement must submit a formal request to their manager and HR for review and approval. Approval is not guaranteed and may be revoked if performance or operational needs require it.

## Equipment and Technology

EKAP provides eligible remote employees with the following standard equipment:

- Company laptop (MacBook Pro or Windows ThinkPad based on role requirements)
- External monitor (27" or equivalent)
- Keyboard and mouse
- Headset for video conferencing

Employees are responsible for maintaining company-provided equipment in good working order and must return all equipment upon separation. Requests for additional equipment must be approved by the employee's manager and submitted through IT.

## Home Office Requirements

Employees working remotely must maintain a dedicated workspace that supports professional and focused work. Requirements include:

- A separate, dedicated area in the home used primarily for work (not a shared family space during work hours)
- A comfortable, ergonomic setup with proper seating, desk height, and monitor positioning
- Reliable high-speed internet connection (minimum 25 Mbps download / 10 Mbps upload recommended)
- A professional, appropriate background for video calls (physical or virtual)

EKAP does not reimburse home office furniture or internet costs except where required by state law. Employees should consult with HR regarding any applicable stipends for their state.

## Security Requirements

Protecting EKAP data is a shared responsibility. Remote employees must adhere to the following security requirements:

- **VPN Usage:** Connection to the EKAP VPN is mandatory when accessing internal systems, shared drives, or any confidential company data. The VPN must remain active throughout the work session.
- **Public Wi-Fi Prohibition:** Employees must never use public Wi-Fi networks (coffee shops, airports, hotels) for work involving sensitive data. Use a personal hotspot or wait until on a secure network.
- **Screen Lock:** Devices must be locked immediately when stepping away from the workspace. Set automatic screen lock to activate after no more than 5 minutes of inactivity.
- **Visitor Confidentiality:** Employees must ensure that sensitive conversations and on-screen data are not visible or audible to household members or visitors.
- **Unauthorized Software:** Installing unapproved software on company devices is prohibited.

Report any suspected security incidents immediately to the IT Security team at security@ekap.com.

## Core Hours and Availability

To support collaboration across time zones and teams, all remote employees are expected to be available and responsive during **core hours: 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the employee's local time zone**. Outside of core hours, employees have flexibility to manage their schedules as agreed with their manager, provided they meet their commitments and deadlines.

Employees must keep their calendars up to date, respond to messages within a reasonable time during their working hours, and participate in all scheduled meetings.

## Hybrid Minimum Requirements

Employees in hybrid arrangements are required to work from an EKAP office location a minimum of **2 days per week**. Specific in-office days may be designated by the team or manager. Employees must be in the office for key collaboration events such as team planning sessions, all-hands meetings, and onboarding activities for new team members.

## Performance Expectations

Remote work is a privilege contingent on sustained performance. Employees working remotely are held to the same performance standards as their on-site peers. Managers will evaluate remote employees based on results, quality of work, and collaboration — not hours logged. Consistent performance issues or failure to adhere to this policy may result in revocation of remote work privileges.

Questions about this policy should be directed to hrteam@ekap.com.
