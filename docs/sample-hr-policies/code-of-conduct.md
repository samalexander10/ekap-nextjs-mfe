# Code of Conduct Policy

## Company Values

EKAP's Code of Conduct is grounded in four core values that guide every decision and interaction:

**Integrity** — We act honestly and transparently in all business dealings. We keep our commitments, admit mistakes, and do the right thing even when no one is watching.

**Respect** — We treat every person with dignity, regardless of their role, background, or identity. We listen actively, value diverse perspectives, and create an environment where everyone can contribute.

**Excellence** — We hold ourselves to the highest standards of quality and professionalism. We continuously seek to improve our skills, processes, and outcomes.

**Innovation** — We embrace creative thinking and welcome new ideas. We support responsible risk-taking and learn from both successes and failures.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict, or appear to conflict, with the interests of EKAP. A conflict of interest arises when personal relationships, financial interests, or outside activities could impair an employee's ability to act in EKAP's best interest.

Employees must disclose actual or potential conflicts of interest to their manager and HR as soon as they become aware of them. Common situations requiring disclosure include: ownership interest in a competitor or supplier, hiring or supervising a family member or romantic partner, accepting gifts or hospitality from vendors valued above $50, and serving on the board of a company that does business with EKAP.

## Confidentiality of Company Information

Employees have access to confidential business information including strategic plans, financial data, customer information, proprietary technology, and personnel records. This information must be protected and may not be disclosed to unauthorized parties during or after employment. Confidentiality obligations survive termination of employment.

Employees must not discuss confidential matters in public places, use personal devices to store sensitive company data without authorization, or share login credentials with others.

## Acceptable Use of Company Assets and Systems

Company assets — including computers, phones, networks, software, vehicles, and office equipment — are provided for business purposes. Limited personal use is acceptable if it does not interfere with work duties, consume excessive resources, or violate this policy. Employees must never use company assets for personal financial gain, to run a competing business, or for any illegal purpose.

All company systems are subject to monitoring in accordance with applicable law. Employees should have no expectation of privacy when using company-owned devices or networks.

## Social Media Guidelines

Employees are responsible for what they post online, even on personal accounts. When discussing EKAP publicly, employees must make clear they are sharing personal opinions, not official company statements. Employees must never share confidential company information, disparage customers or colleagues, or post content that could constitute harassment or discrimination.

## Workplace Violence Prevention

EKAP is committed to a safe workplace free from threats, intimidation, and violence. Threatening language, aggressive behavior, possession of weapons on company property, and any act of violence are grounds for immediate termination and may result in law enforcement involvement.

## Reporting Ethics Violations

Employees who observe conduct that violates this Code of Conduct have a responsibility to report it. Available reporting channels include:

- **Ethics Hotline:** 1-800-EKAP-ETH (1-800-352-7384) — anonymous reporting available 24/7
- **Manager:** Report to your direct manager or any manager you trust
- **HR Department:** Contact hrteam@ekap.com or visit any HR office
- **Legal Department:** For potential legal violations, contact legal@ekap.com

EKAP prohibits retaliation against employees who report ethics violations in good faith. All reports will be investigated promptly and confidentially.
