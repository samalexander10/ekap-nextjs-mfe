# Legal Name Change Process

## Overview

EKAP supports employees who need to update their legal name in company systems following a life event such as marriage, divorce, or a court-ordered name change. This process ensures that all relevant HR, payroll, benefits, and access systems are updated accurately and efficiently. Name changes must be supported by official legal documentation and processed through HR.

## Required Documents

To initiate a legal name change, employees must provide one of the following official documents:

- **Marriage Certificate:** An official, government-issued marriage certificate reflecting the new name. Photocopies or photographs are acceptable if clearly legible; originals are not required to be surrendered.
- **Court Order:** A certified copy of a court order legally changing the employee's name.
- **Notarized Affidavit:** In limited circumstances, a notarized affidavit of name change may be accepted. Please contact HR to confirm eligibility before submitting this document type.

All submitted documents are treated as confidential and retained securely in the employee's HR file in compliance with privacy regulations.

## How to Submit Your Request

Employees have two convenient ways to submit a name change request:

**Option 1 — EKAP HR Chat (Recommended):**
Use the Name Change mini-app within the EKAP HR Chat interface. You will be prompted to enter your current name, new last name, and document type, and to upload a scan or photo of your supporting documentation. The system will generate a request ID for tracking.

**Option 2 — Employee Self-Service Portal:**
Log in to the EKAP Self-Service Portal at selfservice.ekap.internal and navigate to My Profile > Personal Information > Legal Name Change. Complete the form and attach your documentation.

If you experience technical difficulties with either submission method, contact HR directly at hrteam@ekap.com or call HR at 1-800-HR-EKAP.

## Processing Timeline

Name change requests are typically processed within **3 to 5 business days** of submission of complete documentation. Complex situations or high submission volume may extend this timeline; HR will notify affected employees of any delays.

Employees will receive an email confirmation when their request has been received and a second confirmation when processing is complete.

## What Gets Updated

Upon approval and processing of a name change request, the following systems will be updated by HR and the relevant teams:

| System | Updated By | Typical Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Workday (HR System of Record) | HR | Days 1-2 |
| Company Email Address | IT | Days 2-3 |
| Benefits Administration | Benefits Team | Days 2-3 |
| Employee Badge and Physical Access | Facilities/IT | Days 3-5 |
| Payroll Records and Tax Documents | Payroll | Days 3-5 |
| Directory Listings | IT | Days 3-5 |

**Important:** Your Social Security Administration (SSA) records must be updated independently by you before your name can be updated in payroll. Visit ssa.gov or your local SSA office to update your name with the SSA, then provide HR with your updated Social Security card so payroll records can be aligned.

## Employee Responsibilities After Processing

Once your name change has been processed, you are responsible for updating the following independently:

- **Social Security Administration:** Update your SSA records first (see note above)
- **State Driver's License or ID:** Contact your state DMV
- **Passport:** Contact the U.S. Department of State if applicable
- **Bank and Financial Accounts:** Update accounts used for direct deposit
- **Professional Licenses and Certifications:** Contact the relevant licensing bodies
- **Emergency Contact Records:** Update your emergency contact information in Workday
