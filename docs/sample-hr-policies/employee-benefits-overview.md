# Employee Benefits Overview

## Introduction

EKAP is committed to supporting the health, financial security, and wellbeing of our employees and their families. This document provides an overview of the comprehensive benefits package available to all full-time employees. Benefits become effective on the first day of the month following your start date unless otherwise noted.

## Medical Insurance

EKAP offers two medical plan options through our carrier network:

**Preferred Provider Organization (PPO):** Greater flexibility to see any doctor without a referral, including out-of-network providers (at higher cost sharing). Best for employees who value choice and see specialists regularly.

**Health Maintenance Organization (HMO):** Lower out-of-pocket costs when using in-network providers; requires primary care physician referrals for specialists.

EKAP pays 80% of monthly premiums for both plans. Employees are responsible for the remaining 20%. Dependent coverage is available; EKAP contributes 50% toward dependent premiums.

## Dental Insurance

EKAP provides dental coverage including preventive care (100% covered in-network), basic restorative care, and major restorative services. EKAP pays 70% of monthly dental premiums. Orthodontic coverage is included with a lifetime maximum benefit of $2,000 per covered individual.

## Vision Insurance

Vision coverage includes annual eye exams, frames or contact lens allowances, and discounts on laser vision correction. EKAP pays 60% of monthly vision premiums. Employees receive a $200 annual allowance for frames and a $150 allowance for contact lenses.

## 401(k) Retirement Plan

EKAP offers a 401(k) plan to help employees build long-term financial security:

- Employees may contribute up to the IRS annual limit ($23,000 in 2024; $30,500 if age 50 or older)
- EKAP matches 100% of employee contributions up to 6% of eligible compensation
- Employer match vests over a 2-year schedule: 50% after year one, 100% after year two
- A wide range of investment options are available, including target-date funds

## Life and AD&D Insurance

EKAP provides company-paid basic life insurance and accidental death and dismemberment (AD&D) coverage equal to two times your annual base salary, up to a maximum of $500,000. Employees may purchase additional voluntary coverage for themselves and dependents.

## Disability Insurance

**Short-Term Disability:** Provides 60% of base salary replacement for up to 12 weeks following a qualifying disability, after a 7-day waiting period. Company-paid.

**Long-Term Disability:** Provides 60% of base salary replacement for disabilities lasting beyond 12 weeks, up to age 65 or recovery. Company-paid.

## Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA) and Health Savings Account (HSA)

**Health FSA:** Employees enrolled in the PPO may contribute up to $3,200 pre-tax annually to pay for eligible medical, dental, and vision expenses.

**HSA:** Employees enrolled in the High-Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) option may contribute to a Health Savings Account. EKAP contributes $500/year for employee-only coverage and $1,000/year for family coverage.

**Dependent Care FSA:** Contribute up to $5,000 pre-tax annually for eligible dependent care expenses.

## Employee Assistance Program (EAP)

The EAP provides free, confidential support services including 6 free counseling sessions per issue per year, legal consultations, financial counseling, and crisis support. Available 24/7 at 1-800-EAP-HELP (1-800-327-4357).

## Tuition Reimbursement

Employees who have completed one year of continuous employment may apply for tuition reimbursement of up to $5,250 per calendar year for approved degree programs, professional certifications, and job-related courses. Pre-approval from HR and the employee's manager is required.

## Commuter Benefits

Employees may set aside up to $315 per month pre-tax for transit passes and vanpool expenses, and up to $315 per month for qualified parking. Enrollment is available through the benefits portal.

For personalized guidance on benefit elections, contact the HR Benefits Team at benefits@ekap.com or visit the Employee Benefits Portal on the company intranet.
